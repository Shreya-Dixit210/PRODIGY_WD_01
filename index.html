Our Story

Where Every Meal is a Monument

Perched atop Hotel Shanti Lodge in the heart of Tajganj, Chia Taj View Café offers what no other restaurant in Agra can — an unobstructed, panoramic view of the Taj Mahal from the highest vantage point in town.

From the golden hues of sunrise to the silver glow of a moonlit evening, every meal here is framed by one of the world's greatest wonders. We serve a diverse menu of Indian, Chinese, and Japanese dishes crafted with care, paired with exceptional coffee and refreshing beverages.

Whether you're a traveller chasing the perfect sunrise shot or a food lover seeking memorable flavours, our rooftop is where your Agra story begins.
